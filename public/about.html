About Us & Contact

Our Story

Blazin' Wings started right here in Oshkosh, Wisconsin back in 2010 with one mission: bring bold, unforgettable wing flavors to every corner of the state. What began as a small red-and-orange food truck has grown into a local favorite known for its heat, heart, and hometown pride.

We built our menu around fresh ingredients, handcrafted sauces, and a passion for flavor. Whether you’re craving classic mild wings or daring to take on our signature “Inferno Blaze,” every batch is cooked to order and served with a smile.

Over the years, we’ve served thousands of hungry Wisconsinites at festivals, breweries, college events, and community gatherings. We love what we do — and we love the people we serve even more.

What Makes Us Different

🔥 Fresh, never frozen wings: Quality you can taste in every bite.

🔥 House-made sauces: From sweet and smoky to melt-your-face spicy.

🔥 Fast service, friendly faces: We treat every customer like family.

🔥 Locally sourced ingredients: Supporting Wisconsin farms and suppliers.

🔥 Community-focused: We proudly serve at charity events, school fundraisers, and local festivals.

Where You Can Find Us

Our truck travels all across the Fox Valley area — Oshkosh, Appleton, Neenah, Menasha, and beyond. You’ll often spot us at:

  • 🎵 Local music festivals
  • 🍺 Brewery nights and taproom events
  • 🏈 UW Oshkosh Titans games
  • 🎡 County fairs and summer celebrations
  • 🚚 Weekly rotating food truck rallies
